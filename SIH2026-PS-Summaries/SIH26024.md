# SIH26024 — AI-Based Smart Governance & Compliance Monitoring System for Coal Mines

- **Organization:** Ministry of Coal
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 5.5/10

## What the PS is about
Coal mining governance (statutory compliance, inspections, safety observations, production reporting, contractor management, grievances) runs on fragmented manual systems — causing inconsistencies, compliance gaps and no real-time insight.

## What to build
A centralized AI-enabled governance platform that:
- Digitally tracks statutory compliance (safety, environment, production, labour)
- Real-time monitoring of inspections, violations and corrective actions with geo-tagged, time-stamped mobile field reporting (offline capable)
- AI/analytics to flag high-risk areas, recurring failures and anomalies; predictive alerts
- Dashboards for mine officials, corporate management and regulators; automated workflows, escalations and reports
- GIS mapping, OCR digitization, secure audit trails (optionally blockchain)

## Key challenges
Breadth of workflows and stakeholder roles; AI layer is analytical rather than deep.

## Why this difficulty
Big enterprise-platform scope but conventional tech; a strong full-stack team can build a credible prototype quickly.
