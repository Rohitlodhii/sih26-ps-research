# SIH26047 — Patient Case-Taking Software (AI Clinical Intake for Indian OPDs / AYUSH)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Ayush (AIIA)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** MedTech / BioTech / HealthTech
- **Difficulty Score:** 7.5/10

## What the PS is about
Indian OPD doctors get 2–5 minutes per patient; history-taking is rushed, records are fragmented paper documents. Ayurveda needs an even deeper intake (Prakriti, Vikriti, Agni, Koshtha, etc.). ABDM/FHIR infrastructure exists but the "first-mile" structured intake is missing.

## What to build
An AI-powered, patient-facing digital clinical intake platform (kiosk/mobile):
- Multimodal conversational history-taking using ASR for Indian languages/accents (Bhashini/AI4Bharat) + LLM-driven structured elicitation of complaints, history, allergies, review of systems
- Ayurvedic assessment modules (Trividha/Ashtavidha/Dashavidha Pariksha)
- OCR digitization of prior prescriptions/reports (handwritten, multilingual), chronologically organized
- Structured output into ABDM ecosystem (ABHA ID, FHIR) before the doctor walks in
- Doctor-facing summary to compress consultation time

## Key challenges
Medical conversational AI in Indian languages, handwritten OCR, FHIR/ABDM integration, clinical safety of LLM outputs.

## Why this difficulty
Multiple hard AI components (multilingual ASR dialogue, document AI) plus standards integration — high ceiling, strong team needed.
