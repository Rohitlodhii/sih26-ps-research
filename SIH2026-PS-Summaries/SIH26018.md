# SIH26018 — Intelligent Land Record Digitization and Validation System

- **Organization:** Ministry of Rural Development (DoLR)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
Vast amounts of Indian land records exist as handwritten registers, scanned maps and legacy PDFs — often faded, damaged and multilingual — making manual digitization slow and error-prone.

## What to build
An AI platform that:
- Uses OCR + computer vision + NLP to read printed AND handwritten text in multiple Indian languages from scanned records
- Extracts and classifies structured fields: owner details, survey/khasra/khata numbers, plot area, village/tehsil/district, land class, mutation and registration info
- Validates via business rules, cross-database checks, duplicate detection, confidence scoring, and human-in-the-loop verification for low-confidence fields
- Improves accuracy through continual learning
- Integrates with LRMS, DILRMP databases, GIS platforms; audit trails, dashboards, APIs, role-based access

## Key challenges
Handwritten multilingual OCR is genuinely hard (low-resource Indic scripts, degraded documents); field-level extraction accuracy.

## Why this difficulty
The platform work is standard, but the OCR/NLP core on handwritten Indic records is at the frontier of practical document AI.
