# SIH26044 — Portal for Academia–Industry Collaboration: Skill Mapping, Internships & Placement

- **Organization:** Ministry of Ayush
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 4/10

## What the PS is about
A gap exists between academic skills and industry expectations; students can't see required skills, industries can't find matching candidates, and academicians lack industry exposure channels.

## What to build
A one-stop collaboration portal with:
- Skill assessment (questionnaires/aptitude tests) → skill profiles and gap analysis vs industry needs
- Recommendations of industries, roles and skill-development programs
- Industry-posted internships/projects/jobs with student matching and tracking
- Industry learning programs (courses, certifications, mentorship, FDPs for faculty, consultancy, research collaboration)
- Digital student portfolios, placement recommendation engine, institution analytics dashboards, role-based access, secure document management

## Key challenges
Scope breadth and matching/recommendation quality — but all with mainstream tech.

## Why this difficulty
Essentially a well-specified job-portal + LMS-lite build; very approachable.
