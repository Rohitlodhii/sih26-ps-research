# SIH26078 — AI Spatio-Temporal Tracking of Extreme Weather Anomalies in Medium-Range Forecasts (GNN + Diffusion)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 9.5/10

## What the PS is about
Tracking extreme anomalies (cyclones, heat domes) in 12 km global ensemble data is manual and computationally heavy, and standard deep nets blur the extreme peaks forecasters care about (spectral smoothing).

## What to build
A two-stage hybrid AI pipeline:
1. **Spherical GNN tracker:** map NCMRWF NEPS-G ensemble grids onto an icosahedral mesh, compute Extreme Forecast Index vs a 30-year ERA5 baseline, isolate anomalies and output 4D trajectory bounding boxes over 3–10 days
2. **Diffusion downscaler:** conditional denoising diffusion model downscaling the 12 km anomaly slice to 5 km while preserving extreme amplitudes
- Physics-informed loss functions penalizing thermodynamically impossible states
- Stack: PyTorch/JAX, DGL, Hugging Face Diffusers, xarray/Dask, MetPy, Cartopy; validated on historical events (Cyclone Amphan, heatwaves)

## Key challenges
Graph neural networks on spherical meshes, generative downscaling, physics-constrained training, multi-gigabyte 4D data wrangling.

## Why this difficulty
State-of-the-art research engineering (GraphCast/Gencast territory) — the most advanced software PS in SIH 2026; realistic only for exceptional ML teams.
