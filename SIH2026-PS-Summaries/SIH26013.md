# SIH26013 — Automated Integration & Intelligent Harmonization of Multi-source Geospatial Data for Urban Land Records

- **Organization:** Ministry of Rural Development (DoLR)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
Under programs like NAKSHA, huge volumes of geospatial data (drone surveys, ORI, DSM/DTM, GNSS, municipal/revenue records, utility data) are generated — but harmonizing them is manual GIS work that is slow and error-prone.

## What to build
An AI-enabled geospatial integration platform that:
- Auto-integrates, harmonizes, validates and synchronizes multiple land datasets with AI feature-extraction outputs
- Uses ML spatial-matching algorithms, automated topology correction, intelligent attribute mapping
- Handles geo-referencing/coordinate transformations, change detection, spatial conflict resolution, confidence scoring
- Tech: AI/ML, GeoAI, GIS/Web-GIS, spatial databases, ETL automation, computer vision, cloud, APIs

## Key challenges
Spatial entity matching across heterogeneous schemas and coordinate systems; conflict resolution logic.

## Why this difficulty
Complex data-engineering problem with subtle geometry/semantics, but no hardware and well-understood GIS tooling.
