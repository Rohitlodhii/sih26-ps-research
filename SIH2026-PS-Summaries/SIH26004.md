# SIH26004 — AI-Assisted Early Detection System for Osteoarthritis (OA) Risk Markers in NER

- **Organization:** Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MDoNER)
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** MedTech / BioTech / HealthTech
- **Difficulty Score:** 8/10

## What the PS is about
Osteoarthritis is widespread among elderly and physically active people in NER, but rural facilities lack affordable screening tools and orthopaedic specialists. Early OA risk detection is missing in low-resource settings.

## What to build
An AI-assisted OA screening system for health workers that:
- Detects early risk markers via joint-movement analysis, gait/posture assessment, pain & mobility inputs, and sensor/imaging-based assessment
- Uses AI/ML to flag high-risk cases and indicate severity
- Works in primary health centres, rural camps and outreach programs
- Digital patient records + report generation
- Multilingual, simple UI; offline data collection with sync
- Portable, affordable hardware; includes preventive-care guidance

## Key challenges
Building reliable sensing hardware (gait/joint/imaging), validating medical-adjacent AI, and deploying in low-resource field conditions.

## Why this difficulty
Combines hardware prototyping, medical AI and clinical credibility — hardware accuracy and validation are genuinely hard.
