# SIH26006 — Intelligent Freight Forecasting Model for Vessel Chartering & Bulk Cargo Procurement (East Coast India)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Steel
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Transportation & Logistics
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
Vessel chartering for bulk cargo (coal, ore) to East Coast ports (Paradip, Vizag, Dhamra, Haldia, etc.) is reactive — teams scout the volatile global freight market daily, missing optimal contract windows and choosing suboptimal vessel sizes given port draft/berthing limits.

## What to build
A data-driven freight forecasting and decision-support model that:
- Predicts future freight rates per vessel class (Handysize, Supramax, Panamax, Capesize) and trade route using time-series ML/regression
- Recommends optimal market-entry timing for short/mid-term charters
- Recommends the best vessel type per cargo parcel considering port constraints (LOA, beam, draft, handling rates) at origin (Australia, US, Mozambique, Indonesia, Russia) and destination
- Integrates historical freight rates, economic indicators, commodity prices, seasonality, and real-time port congestion

## Key challenges
Acquiring quality global freight-rate and port-congestion data, niche maritime domain knowledge, and forecasting accuracy in a volatile market.

## Why this difficulty
The ML itself (time-series) is standard, but data procurement and the specialized domain modeling make it demanding.
