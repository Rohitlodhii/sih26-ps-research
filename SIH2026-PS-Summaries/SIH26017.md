# SIH26017 — Predictive Analytics System for Early Detection of Land Acquisition Delays

- **Organization:** Ministry of Rural Development (DoLR)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 5.5/10

## What the PS is about
Land acquisition delays derail infrastructure projects. Causes range from approval bottlenecks and legal disputes to compensation delays and R&R issues — but there is no mechanism to predict delays before they happen.

## What to build
An AI-powered predictive decision-support platform that:
- Learns from historical/current land-acquisition cases (project type, area, affected families, compensation status, approval timelines, disputes, possession, stakeholder responsiveness)
- Generates per-project risk scores and delay probabilities per lifecycle stage
- Explains key delay drivers (explainable AI) and recommends mitigations
- Dashboards for high-risk projects, district/state delay trends, GIS visualization, automated alerts
- Continuous model retraining; APIs into existing acquisition systems; role-based access + audit trails

## Key challenges
Getting realistic historical project data; keeping models honest and explainable.

## Why this difficulty
Classic tabular ML + dashboard build; conceptually simple, data access is the real hurdle, hence moderate difficulty.
