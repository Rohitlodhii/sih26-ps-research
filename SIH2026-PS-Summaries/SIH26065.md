# SIH26065 — Autonomous Low-Cost Ocean Observation Platform for Polar & Southern Oceans

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Robotics and Drones
- **Difficulty Score:** 8/10

## What the PS is about
Polar and Southern Ocean observation (buoys/gliders) is expensive; India needs an indigenous, low-cost autonomous platform for long-term measurement of oceanographic and atmospheric parameters in ice-infested, stormy waters.

## What to build
An autonomous ocean observation platform that:
- Deploys long-term in harsh polar environments
- Measures key oceanographic + atmospheric parameters (temperature, salinity, currents, met variables)
- Operates autonomously with reliable data telemetry and endurance

## Key challenges
Ice/survivability engineering, battery endurance for months-long missions, satellite telemetry, anti-fouling/icing.

## Why this difficulty
Real ocean-going hardware with polar survivability — excellent but demanding marine engineering; prototype-level demos are achievable though.
