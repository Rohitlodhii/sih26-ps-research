# SIH26059 — AI-Enabled Antarctic Sea-Ice, Iceberg Trajectory & Navigation Decision Support System (MoES)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Transportation & Logistics
- **Difficulty Score:** 7.5/10

## What the PS is about
Indian research vessels navigating to/in Antarctica need forecasts of sea-ice concentration, iceberg drift trajectories and safe, fuel-efficient routes based on satellite, oceanographic and meteorological data.

## What to build
An AI/ML decision-support platform that:
- Forecasts Antarctic sea-ice concentration (satellite-derived, e.g., passive microwave data)
- Predicts iceberg trajectories from ocean current/wind/sea-state models
- Plans safe and fuel-efficient navigation routes combining the above with vessel constraints
- Presents actionable outputs for voyage planning

## Key challenges
Geospatial/spatiotemporal ML on satellite datasets, blending physical ocean models with ML, scarce labels for iceberg drift.

## Why this difficulty
Research-flavored geospatial forecasting with big public datasets (NSIDC, Copernicus) — doable but requires genuine remote-sensing + ML competence.
