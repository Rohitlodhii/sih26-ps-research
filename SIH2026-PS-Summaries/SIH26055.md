# SIH26055 — Smart Scan Strategy for Electronic Warfare (DRDO)

- **Organization:** DRDO
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Robotics and Drones
- **Difficulty Score:** 8.5/10

## What the PS is about
EW receivers must sweep huge spectra with narrow instantaneous bandwidth. Open-loop (pre-mission) scan strategies waste time on non-threats and miss new/frequency-agile emitters. Interception is a 2D search problem: choosing the right frequency at the right time.

## What to build
An ML-based electronic-support receiver scheduler:
- System model of the receiver + simulated RF environment with ground truth per band/time slot (transmitting or not), including spatially scanning and frequency-agile emitters
- Figures of merit: probability of detection/false alarm, sensitivity, average intercept rate, reward/cost, prediction accuracy, intercept-time error
- ML scheduler trained on hits/misses to minimize intercept time and maximize interception rate
- Optimal strategies against periodic-scan receivers; prediction of intercept time/ratio

## Key challenges
Highly specialized RF/EW domain, building a faithful simulation environment, reinforcement/sequential-decision formulation.

## Why this difficulty
Niche defence problem with no off-the-shelf references; strong grasp of probability, scheduling theory and ML required. Conceptually deep even though it's pure software.
