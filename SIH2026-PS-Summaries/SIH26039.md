# SIH26039 — AI-Powered Underground Mine Safety, Monitoring & Rescue System (Jharkhand)

- **Organization:** Government of Jharkhand
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 8/10

## What the PS is about
Jharkhand's underground coal mines face toxic gas leaks, collapses, flooding and poor visibility; rescue teams go in blind, increasing risk and delaying response.

## What to build
A rugged rescue rover (or compact aerial drone) that:
- Carries gas sensors, thermal/night-vision cameras, environmental sensors, wireless comms
- Streams live video + thermal imaging to a surface control station
- Detects toxic gases, monitors temperature/humidity/structural conditions
- Explores inaccessible areas, detects hazards, helps locate trapped workers; AI-based analysis and remote monitoring

## Key challenges
Underground navigation (GPS-denied, dust, water), robust wireless communication through rock/tunnels, explosion-proof considerations, ruggedization.

## Why this difficulty
A real field robot for hostile environments — communication underground is the classic hard part, plus gas sensing and navigation. Demo-able with a prototype, but genuinely demanding.
