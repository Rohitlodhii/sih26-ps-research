# SIH26002 — AI-Based Smart Logistics & Accessibility Intelligence Platform for NER

- **Organization:** Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MDoNER)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Transportation & Logistics
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
Difficult terrain, landslides and floods disrupt transport of essential goods (medicines, food, materials) to remote NER districts. There is no integrated platform for real-time logistics visibility, route status and disruption prediction.

## What to build
An AI logistics intelligence platform that:
- Monitors real-time road/bridge accessibility across districts
- Predicts route disruptions (landslides, floods, congestion) and suggests alternate routes with delay estimates
- Tracks vehicles carrying essential commodities via GPS
- Sends automated alerts for blocked roads, delays, high-risk corridors
- Lets field officials upload geo-tagged incident reports
- Central dashboard: district connectivity, supply-chain bottlenecks, emergency routes
- Multilingual + offline sync

## Key challenges
Route-disruption prediction modeling, GPS fleet tracking at scale, integration with weather/transport databases, offline support.

## Why this difficulty
Standard tech stack but very broad feature set; predictive disruption alerts are the hard, data-hungry part.
