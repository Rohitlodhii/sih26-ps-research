# SIH26080 — Regime-Aware AI Post-Processing of Monsoon Rainfall Forecasts

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
NWP rainfall bias varies by weather regime (active/break monsoon, depressions, orographic/coastal rain, western disturbances) — a single bias-correction fails everywhere.

## What to build
A regime-aware post-processing system that:
- Classifies the prevailing weather regime first
- Applies regime-appropriate AI bias correction to raw NWP rainfall forecasts
- Outputs district-level corrected rainfall, heavy-rainfall exceedance probabilities, and a verification report (RMSE, ETS, CSI, POD, FAR, FSS)

## Key challenges
Regime classification robustness, correction models per regime, rigorous verification metrics.

## Why this difficulty
Clearly structured ML pipeline with named evaluation criteria; moderate-heavy but very tractable with IMD data.
