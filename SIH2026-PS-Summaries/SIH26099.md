# SIH26099 — AI Standardization & Harmonization of Material Codes Across CPSEs ("One Nation One Material Code")

- **Organization:** Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 6/10

## What the PS is about
CPSEs (Oil & Gas, Power, Steel, Mining) each code identical materials differently — duplicate masters, inconsistent specs, fragmented procurement, excess inventory, no collaborative purchasing.

## What to build
An AI National Unified Material Master Framework that:
- Analyzes material codes, descriptions, specs and procurement history from multiple CPSE ERP/SAP systems
- Uses NLP/ML to find identical, duplicate, near-duplicate and functionally equivalent materials
- Recommends standardized descriptions, classifications and a Common National Material Code, mapped back to each CPSE's codes
- Human-in-the-loop validation/approval workflow; legacy migration support; SAP/ERP integration; analytics dashboard, audit trail and governance

## Key challenges
Entity-matching NLP across messy multi-industry catalogs, scale, ERP integration realism.

## Why this difficulty
A focused entity-resolution/data-quality problem — well-understood techniques, good datasets can be synthesized; moderate build.
