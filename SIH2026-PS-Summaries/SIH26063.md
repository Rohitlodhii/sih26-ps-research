# SIH26063 — Integrated Polar Science Outreach, Knowledge Repository & Media Dissemination Portal

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Education
- **Difficulty Score:** 3.5/10

## What the PS is about
Expedition reports, datasets, publications, photos and videos exist in scattered formats; there is no unified public outreach archive.

## What to build
A comprehensive outreach portal that:
- Archives expedition reports, scientific datasets, publications, photographs, videos and institutional activities
- Generates content for websites and social media

## Key challenges
Content management and media handling only.

## Why this difficulty
A CMS-style archive portal — the most approachable PS in the MoES cluster.
