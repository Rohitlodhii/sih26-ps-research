# SIH26033 — Direct Farmer-to-Consumer Digital Marketplace (Eliminating Intermediaries)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Difficulty Score:** 5/10

## What the PS is about
Multiple intermediaries between farmers and consumers cut farmer earnings and raise consumer prices.

## What to build
A digital marketplace that:
- Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers
- Provides logistics support integration
- Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization

## Key challenges
Marketplace plumbing is standard; the "AI" parts (demand forecasting, route optimization) need data and real modeling; logistics partnerships are a business problem more than a tech one.

## Why this difficulty
Comfortable full-stack scope; add light ML for forecasting/optimization to be competitive.
