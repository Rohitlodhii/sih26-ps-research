# SIH26050 — High-Altitude Performance Optimization & Robust Design of Anti-Drone System (DRDO)

- **Organization:** DRDO
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Robotics and Drones
- **Difficulty Score:** 9/10

## What the PS is about
Anti-drone systems (detect/track/identify/neutralize) are tuned for standard conditions; extreme cold, low air density, dust, snow and wind degrade RF, electro-optical, mechanical and stabilization subsystems — critical for systems needing micro-radian pointing accuracy.

## What to build
A high-altitude-capable anti-drone system architecture:
- Environmental hardening/ruggedization for cold, low pressure, dust, high wind
- Component selection/qualification for harsh environments; compensation mechanisms for temperature-induced cable rigidity, component derating, structural deformation, wind disturbance, sensor drift
- Thermal management and protective packaging; adaptive control algorithms, health monitoring and predictive performance assessment
- Modeling/simulation + field evaluation methodology demonstrating detection/tracking/engagement with minimal degradation at altitude

## Key challenges
RF/EO hardware, precision stabilization, neutralization subsystems, and environmental qualification — defence-grade system integration.

## Why this difficulty
A complete defence hardware system with extreme-precision requirements; students can realistically deliver design methodology + simulation + partial subsystem prototype at most.
