# SIH26093 — AI Real-Time Stress & Trauma Assessment Module for NHAA (14566) Victims

- **Organization:** Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (MoSJE)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** MedTech / BioTech / HealthTech
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
SC/ST victims contacting the National Helpline Against Atrocities (14566) show severe distress, but first responders have no standardized way to assess psychological vulnerability at first contact.

## What to build
An AI assessment module integrated with helpline/portal/chatbot/IVRS that:
- Analyzes voice (speech patterns, pauses, pitch, emotion) and text narratives using NLP, speech analytics, Emotion AI
- Generates a Stress Vulnerability Index (SVI) and Low/Moderate/High/Critical risk categories
- Detects trauma indicators including depression, suicidal ideation, intimidation, social isolation
- Auto-recommends counselling, legal aid, medical help, police intervention, witness protection or emergency support by risk level
- Multilingual + dialect support; strict privacy, informed consent, confidentiality and ethical-AI standards

## Key challenges
Emotion/voice-stress AI accuracy across Indian languages, ethical safeguards, false-positive sensitivity around suicide risk.

## Why this difficulty
Affect-sensing ML is doable with open models, but deploying it responsibly for vulnerable victims raises the bar well beyond a tech demo.
