# SIH26094 — AI Dynamic Mental-Health Monitoring & Distress Prediction for Victims of Atrocities

- **Organization:** Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (MoSJE)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** MedTech / BioTech / HealthTech
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
Victims of atrocities suffer prolonged distress through investigation, trial and rehabilitation, but support systems only react at crisis point — no continuous well-being monitoring exists.

## What to build
A longitudinal monitoring & prediction system that:
- Conducts periodic check-ins via chatbot, IVRS calls, SMS, app or helpline follow-ups
- Analyzes voice, text, behaviour and engagement patterns (NLP, sentiment, Emotion AI)
- Maintains a Dynamic Distress Score with trend analysis; predicts escalation before crisis
- Triggers alerts to counsellors/district authorities at risk thresholds; recommends interventions (counselling, witness protection, relocation, compensation, legal aid)
- District/state/national dashboards; explainable AI, privacy and legal compliance

## Key challenges
Longitudinal modeling on sparse check-in data, crisis-prediction reliability, ethics.

## Why this difficulty
Companion to 26093 with a time-series twist; same ethical weight plus predictive validation difficulty.
