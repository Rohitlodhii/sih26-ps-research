# SIH26097 — AI Voice Assistant for Livelihood Mapping & NSQF Skilling Recommendations (PM-AJAY GIA, SC Communities)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (MoSJE)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Difficulty Score:** 6.5/10

## What the PS is about
Under PM-AJAY's Grant-in-Aid, SC beneficiaries get skill training, but program matching fails: text-heavy systems, language barriers, low digital literacy → wrong course enrollment, high dropout, poor employment outcomes.

## What to build
An AI-driven, multilingual, voice-based virtual livelihood assistant that:
- Conducts empathetic conversational interviews (education, family occupations, skills, interests, mobility constraints, self-employment vs wage preference, local economy) instead of forms
- AI/ML profiles the beneficiary to recommend NSQF-aligned training programs, trades, skill gaps and region-specific employment/enterprise opportunities
- Works on low-connectivity, low-tech channels: IVR phone calls for feature phones, WhatsApp voice notes, lightweight kiosks/mobile
- Regional languages and dialects throughout

## Key challenges
Voice interfaces in many dialects for low-literacy users, IVR integration, recommendation grounding in real local market data.

## Why this difficulty
Speech tech is available (Bhashini/AI4Bharat), but multi-channel (IVR/WhatsApp) deployment and empathetic conversation design take real work.
