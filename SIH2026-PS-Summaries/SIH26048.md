# SIH26048 — iKwath: Pod-Based Smart Kwatha (Kadha) Maker Standardized to AFI/API

- **Organization:** Ministry of Ayush
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** MedTech / BioTech / HealthTech
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
Fresh Ayurvedic decoctions (kwatha/kadha) are most effective but laborious (soak, boil at mild heat, reduce to ¼, filter) and must be consumed within hours; shelf-stable concentrates lose potency and invite adulteration. No appliance automates pharmacopoeia-grade preparation.

## What to build
A countertop, pod-based kwatha appliance:
- Single-dose pods of standardized coarse powder (yavakuta churna) acting as brew-bag; changing pod changes formulation
- User adds water + pod; machine soaks, boils at controlled ~85–90 °C, reduces ~400 mL to a ~100 mL dose, filters, dispenses fresh
- Reads formulation profile (water ratio, boil profile) encoded on the pod; monitors weight/extract density for dose consistency
- Fast cycle time WITHOUT altering phytochemical profile/extractive yield vs classical preparation (validated); easy cleaning (dishwasher-safe chamber, rinse cycle, disposable pod), anti-boil-over and dry-run protection, food-grade SS contact

## Key challenges
Precision thermal/evaporation engineering that provably matches classical decoction quality; pod system; food safety.

## Why this difficulty
A real mechatronics + food-science product with a validation requirement (qualitative + quantitative equivalence) — significant prototype iteration.
