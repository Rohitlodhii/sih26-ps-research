# SIH26089 — Cooperative Gig Services Platform for Household & Community Services

- **Organization:** Ministry of Cooperation
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Difficulty Score:** 4.5/10

## What the PS is about
Labour cooperative federations hold thousands of skilled workers (electricians, plumbers, caregivers, drivers…) but lack a digital marketplace — private gig platforms dominate while cooperative workers stay underutilized.

## What to build
A cooperative-owned services marketplace that:
- Registers/verifies service providers; worker skill profiling and certification
- Customer booking/scheduling with geo-location matching; on-demand and emergency bookings
- Digital payments and invoicing; ratings and feedback
- Worker welfare/insurance integration; federation admin dashboard; multilingual mobile app
- AI demand forecasting and workforce allocation

## Key challenges
Standard marketplace build; light ML for matching/forecasting.

## Why this difficulty
A well-understood "Uber-for-cooperative-workers" app; approachable full-stack scope.
