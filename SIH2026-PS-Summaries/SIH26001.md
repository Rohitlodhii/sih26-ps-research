# SIH26001 — AI-Based Early Warning and Landslide Risk Monitoring System in NER

- **Organization:** Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MDoNER)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Disaster Management
- **Difficulty Score:** 7.5/10

## What the PS is about
The North Eastern Region suffers frequent landslides, flash floods and slope failures due to heavy rain and fragile terrain. Monitoring today is reactive and manual, with no real-time prediction of high-risk zones.

## What to build
An AI-powered early-warning platform that:
- Ingests rainfall, soil-moisture sensor, satellite, terrain/slope and historical landslide data
- Uses AI/ML to flag high-risk zones and predict landslide events
- Sends real-time alerts (SMS/app) to district administration and communities
- GIS dashboard with risk heatmaps, road connectivity status, weather-linked forecasts
- Mobile/web app for citizens and field officials to upload geo-tagged photos/videos
- Multilingual notifications + offline sync for low-network areas

## Key challenges
Integrating heterogeneous data sources (IMD APIs, satellite feeds, sensors), building credible predictive models for sparse landslide data, and offline-first architecture for remote regions.

## Why this difficulty
Technically achievable with existing GIS/ML stacks, but real predictive accuracy needs good historical data, and the integration + offline scope is wide.
