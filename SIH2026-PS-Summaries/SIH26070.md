# SIH26070 — AI/ML Identification, Classification & Prediction of Tropical Cyclone Patterns from Multi-Source Satellite Data

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Disaster Management
- **Difficulty Score:** 7.5/10

## What the PS is about
Cyclone detection, intensification and track prediction still rely on manual interpretation plus physics models; multi-source satellite data (INSAT, microwave) can be exploited with AI.

## What to build
An AI/ML system that:
- Identifies and classifies tropical cyclone patterns from multi-source satellite imagery
- Predicts cyclone behavior/patterns (intensity/track evolution)
- Provides operational output usable by forecasters

## Key challenges
Cyclone datasets (IBTrACS + satellite imagery) engineering, spatiotemporal modeling, rare-event class balance.

## Why this difficulty
Well-researched problem area (many published baselines) with public datasets, but building a robust multi-source system with prediction skill is substantial.
