# SIH26084 — Convective-Scale Nowcasting for Thunderstorms, Hail & Cloudbursts (0–6 h, 1–3 km)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Disaster Management
- **Difficulty Score:** 8.5/10

## What the PS is about
Convective storms (hail, downbursts, cloudbursts) form in minutes at scales coarser NWP can't resolve; India lacks operational 1–3 km, 0–6 h nowcasting.

## What to build
A real-time multi-source fusion nowcasting system that:
- Ingests Doppler weather radar (reflectivity/velocity), INSAT-3D/3DR satellite thermal/IR bands, and ground lightning networks
- Detects early convective initiation automatically
- Forecasts severe parameters: lightning strike density, hail probability, downburst velocity, cloudburst thresholds
- GIS dashboard with 1–3 km hazard zones and live storm-arrival countdowns

## Key challenges
High-frequency heterogeneous data fusion in real time, rare severe-event training data, hyper-local validation.

## Why this difficulty
One of the most demanding MoES nowcasting PS — big-data pipelines plus cutting-edge spatiotemporal DL.
