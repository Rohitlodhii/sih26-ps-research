# SIH26009 — Using AI/ML and Space Technology to Identify Manganese Reserves & Overcome Production Shortfalls

- **Organization:** Ministry of Steel (MOIL Ltd.)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Space Technology
- **Difficulty Score:** 7.5/10

## What the PS is about
MOIL (India's largest manganese producer) estimates reserves via manual surveys and drilling — slow and often mismatched with actual production, risking shortfalls.

## What to build
An AI/ML solution that:
- Maps manganese reserves more accurately using surface/sub-surface indicators plus satellite inputs (rainfall, soil moisture, vegetation index, land temperature)
- Predicts production shortfalls from equipment downtime, weather, blasting delays
- Suggests corrective actions (schedule adjustments, blasting optimization, equipment redeployment)
- Delivers a user-friendly dashboard: predicted reserves, production trends, shortfall risk, recommendations

## Key challenges
Geological/remote-sensing expertise, scarce labeled reserve data, fusing satellite + drilling + production data into a credible model.

## Why this difficulty
Highly domain-specific geoscience ML with little open training data; the dashboard itself is easy, the predictive core is research-grade.
