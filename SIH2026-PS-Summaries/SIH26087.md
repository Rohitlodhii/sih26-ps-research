# SIH26087 — AI & LMS-Enabled Cooperative Capacity Building, ERP & Employment Ecosystem (NCCT)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Cooperation (NCCT)
- **Category:** Hardware (listed) | **Theme:** Smart Education
- **Difficulty Score:** 5.5/10

## What the PS is about
NCCT institutions (VAMNICOM, RICMs, ICMs) train lakhs of cooperative personnel, SHGs and rural youth, but training management is manual/fragmented — poor tracking, no employment linkage.

## What to build
An integrated digital ecosystem combining ERP + LMS + employment exchange:
- Online programme registration/nomination; participant & institution profiles
- Multilingual e-learning modules; face-recognition/QR digital attendance
- Timetable, hostel, logistics management; LMS assessments and certification
- Skill certification repository with verification; career-counseling chatbot; employer/recruiter dashboards
- Mobile-friendly, offline-capable learning; centralized analytics database

## Key challenges
Scope breadth (ERP + LMS + recruitment), face recognition attendance integration.

## Why this difficulty
Everything is known technology; the challenge is integration discipline across many modules.
