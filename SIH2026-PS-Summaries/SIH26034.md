# SIH26034 — Compliance Checking of Packaged Commodities under Legal Metrology (PC) Rules, 2011

- **Organization:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Miscellaneous
- **Difficulty Score:** 5.5/10

## What the PS is about
Every packaged commodity must carry mandatory declarations (manufacturer, net quantity, MRP, dates, consumer care). Manual enforcement checks are slow; violations like missing declarations, wrong font sizes and improper MRP are common.

## What to build
A web/mobile app that:
- Scans product label/package images (and e-commerce listings)
- OCR-extracts mandatory declarations and validates correctness, completeness and placement per the Rules
- Checks font size/readability requirements
- Flags missing, misleading or non-standard declarations
- Generates compliance reports (PDF + editable), keeps a scanned-product repository, and gives enforcement dashboards; role-based access

## Key challenges
OCR on diverse packaging (curved surfaces, stylized fonts, vernacular text), encoding the rules into validation logic, font-size measurement from images.

## Why this difficulty
Document AI + a detailed rules engine; tractable, but real-world label image robustness is the crux.
