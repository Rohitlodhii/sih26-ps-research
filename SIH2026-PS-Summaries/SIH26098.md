# SIH26098 — Low-Cost Precision Guidance & Smart Electronic Fuze for a 155 mm Artillery Shell (Yantra India)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Defence (Yantra India Limited)
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Smart Vehicles
- **Difficulty Score:** 10/10

## What the PS is about
Unguided 155 mm shells have high Circular Error Probable (CEP). Converting existing shells into precision-guided munitions via an add-on guidance + fuze kit would cut ammunition waste and collateral damage.

## What to build
A complete precision-guidance kit integrating:
- Canard Actuation Assembly (CAA) for in-flight trajectory correction (with CAD model)
- GNC system with flight-control algorithms achieving CEP ≤ 30 m
- Multi-mode electronic fuze (proximity, time, impact) with pre-fire programming interface
- Sensor suite (IMU, GNSS, barometric, proximity), embedded electronics, power management surviving ~15,000 g launch, spin, vibration and harsh environments
- Simulation of trajectory correction; SWaP-C-optimized, modular, manufacturable design; prototype preferred

## Key challenges
Gun-hardened electronics at extreme launch g-forces, GNC under spin, fuze safety/arming logic, defence-grade reliability — plus controlled-munitions domain knowledge.

## Why this difficulty
The hardest PS in SIH 2026: a precision-guided munitions kit. Realistically a design/architecture + simulation deliverable; physical prototyping is beyond typical student capability and access.
