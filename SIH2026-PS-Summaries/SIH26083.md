# SIH26083 — Extreme Heatwave Early Warning & Human Thermal Stress Index

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Disaster Management
- **Difficulty Score:** 6.5/10

## What the PS is about
Heatwave warnings use raw dry-bulb temperature, ignoring humidity, wind and radiation — the actual physiological killer. Cities need impact-based ("what the weather will do") heat warnings.

## What to build
A predictive platform that:
- Computes proper heat-stress metrics: WBGT, UTCI or Heat Index
- Integrates health/demographic data (elderly, outdoor-worker density) to predict heat-induced mortality/hospitalization spikes 3–5 days ahead
- GIS dashboard with ward-level color-coded alerts and automated public-health advisories
- API pushing SMS/WhatsApp alerts and triggering heat-action plans (cooling centers, power-grid adjustments, outdoor work-hour shifts)

## Key challenges
Data fusion (weather + health + demographics), mortality-model validation, ward-level downscaling.

## Why this difficulty
Impact-based forecasting combines standard meteorology, index formulas and light ML — demanding in data assembly rather than algorithms.
