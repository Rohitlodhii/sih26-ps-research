# SIH26066 — OceanEmbed: Deep-Learning Reconstruction of Subsurface Ocean Temperature from Surface Satellite Observations

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES/INCOIS)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Disaster Management
- **Difficulty Score:** 8.5/10

## What the PS is about
Subsurface ocean temperature is sparse (only ARGO floats, gliders, ships), yet crucial for climate, marine heatwaves and fisheries. Surface satellite variables (SST, SSS, SSH/SLA, currents, winds) carry indirect signatures of subsurface structure.

## What to build
A satellite-embedding deep-learning framework for the North Indian Ocean (0.25°, daily):
- Preprocessing pipeline harmonizing multi-source satellite + ocean data at 0.25° × daily resolution
- Embedding engines (CNN / ViT / autoencoders / GNN / attention hybrids) learning latent ocean representations
- Reconstruction of temperature at 15 standard depths (0–1000 m)
- Validation against independent gridded ARGO (INCOIS LAS) with correlation/RMSE/bias; targets: GLORYS reanalysis for training
- PoC demonstrated over Bay of Bengal / Arabian Sea

## Key challenges
Spatiotemporal DL at scale, physical plausibility of reconstructions, dataset engineering (NetCDF/GRIB pipelines).

## Why this difficulty
Research-grade geoscience deep learning with well-specified datasets — demanding but clearly guided; great for strong ML teams.
