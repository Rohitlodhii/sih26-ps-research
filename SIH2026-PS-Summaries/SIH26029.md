# SIH26029 — Automated High-Current Short-Circuit Test System for IEC 60898-1:2015 MCB Compliance

- **Organization:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 7.5/10

## What the PS is about
MCBs must pass short-circuit breaking-capacity tests (up to 10,000 A) per IEC 60898-1:2015. Current manual/semi-automated testing has imprecise R/XL circuit configurations, long test times, and safety risks for personnel.

## What to build
An automated test machine for single-pole, SPN, DP, TP and FP MCBs (0.5–63 A) that:
- Automatically switches high-power R/XL (resistive/inductive) banks for precise power-factor control
- Transformer-based high-current source delivering up to 10,000 A
- Universal MCB mounting station with arc chute for safety
- PLC/industrial-PC control & data acquisition capturing high-speed waveforms and computing Ip, I²t
- HMI for parameter entry and automatic report generation; comprehensive safety interlocks

## Key challenges
High-power (10 kA) electrical engineering, precise impedance switching, high-speed measurement, safety systems — energy-hazardous lab-grade equipment.

## Why this difficulty
Serious power hardware with standards compliance and personnel-safety stakes; beyond typical student electronics comfort unless the team has strong electrical engineering.
