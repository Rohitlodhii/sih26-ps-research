# SIH26003 — AI-Based Cognitive Gaming & Memory Assistance Platform for Elderly Dementia Patients (NER)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MDoNER)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** MedTech / BioTech / HealthTech
- **Difficulty Score:** 5.5/10

## What the PS is about
Dementia is rising among the elderly in NER, but remote areas lack access to neurological care and cognitive therapy. Families and caregivers have no affordable, culturally tailored digital therapeutic tool.

## What to build
An AI-powered cognitive gaming and memory-assistance platform that:
- Offers interactive games targeting memory, attention, daily-routine recall, pattern recognition
- Adapts difficulty using AI/ML based on patient performance
- Voice-enabled, multilingual UI with culturally familiar themes for elderly users
- Reminders for medicines, hydration, activities, appointments
- Caregiver/health-worker dashboard for progress monitoring and alerts
- Works offline / low connectivity on mobile/tablet; secure patient data storage

## Key challenges
Elderly-friendly UX, adaptive difficulty logic, multilingual voice interaction, offline sync. No heavy hardware or clinical-grade modeling required.

## Why this difficulty
Mostly app + light ML; the main work is thoughtful UX design and content (games/reminders), making it one of the more approachable PS.
