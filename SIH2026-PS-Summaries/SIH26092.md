# SIH26092 — AI-Driven Scheme Matching for Marginalized Entrepreneurs (SC Concessional Credit)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (MoSJE/NSFDC ecosystem)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 4.5/10

## What the PS is about
SC beneficiaries (income ≤ ₹5L) can get 90% funding at 6.5–8% via 100+ Channel Partners (SCAs, PSBs, RRBs, NBFC-MFIs) — but don't know which scheme fits (Micro Finance ≤₹1.40L, Term Loan ≤₹50L, Educational Loan) or where their nearest eligible partner is.

## What to build
A multilingual platform/app with:
1. **Smart scheme recommender:** rule/AI engine over project type, cost, income, education status
2. **Financial calculator:** EMIs with scheme-specific limits, rates (6.5–15%), moratoria (3–12 months)
3. **Geo-spatial partner locator & router:** nearest eligible Channel Partner by location and fund-utilization eligibility (avoiding high-NPA partners)

## Key challenges
Minimal — mostly rules engine + maps + calculator.

## Why this difficulty
Small, well-specified utility platform; great beginner PS.
