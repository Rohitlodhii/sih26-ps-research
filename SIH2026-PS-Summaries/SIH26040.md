# SIH26040 — Smart Water Purification & Quality Monitoring System (Rural/Mining-Affected Areas)

- **Organization:** Government of Jharkhand
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Clean & Green Technology
- **Difficulty Score:** 5.5/10

## What the PS is about
Rural and mining-affected Jharkhand suffers contaminated groundwater (particles, minerals, microbes, mining pollutants) with no real-time water-quality monitoring.

## What to build
A smart, cost-effective, portable purification unit that:
- Continuously monitors pH, turbidity, TDS, temperature
- Uses multi-stage filtration/purification to remove contaminants and pathogens
- IoT-based real-time monitoring, insights and alerts for unsafe water
- Energy-efficient, easy to deploy in remote low-infrastructure areas

## Key challenges
Sensor integration (cheap pH/TDS/turbidity probes), designing an actual purification chain that works on local contaminants, durability.

## Why this difficulty
Well-known component stack; the purifier itself can be assembled from existing filtration stages — the work is integration, monitoring and cost engineering.
