# SIH26095 — Smart Real-Time Monitoring & Inspection Mobile App (DoSJE Schemes)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (MoSJE)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 5/10

## What the PS is about
NGO/institute projects under DoSJE schemes suffer fake reporting and proxy functioning; the department lacks real-time oversight tools.

## What to build
A centralized monitoring app with:
- Live CCTV feed integration from projects/institutes
- Random video-conferencing (VC) connectivity with project in-charges/staff/beneficiaries
- Real-time monitoring dashboard for officials; mobile inspection module for PMU teams
- AI/randomized assignment of inspection duties
- Geo-tagged inspection reports with live evidence capture
- AI anomaly and attendance analytics

## Key challenges
CCTV/RTSP integration across heterogeneous setups, randomized scheduling logic, attendance anomaly detection.

## Why this difficulty
Integration-heavy but standard components; a solid mid-level build.
