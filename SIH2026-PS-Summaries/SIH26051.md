# SIH26051 — Software Model for Area-Specific Passive Shelter Design for Thermal Comfort (DRDO, Ladakh)

- **Organization:** DRDO
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Miscellaneous
- **Difficulty Score:** 6/10

## What the PS is about
Ladakh shelters lose heat rapidly after sunset despite 1900–2100 kWh/m²/yr solar irradiance and 300+ clear days. Region-specific passive shelters could cut fossil-fuel heating, but no general design tool exists.

## What to build
A user-friendly ANSYS-based thermal simulation model that:
- Accepts user inputs: real ambient climate data, shelter size/shape/orientation, material properties, thermal-mass storage materials, openings
- Predicts inside temperature over time, solar thermal energy capture, and heat-flow vs ambient for defined periods
- Compares material/design combinations to recommend the most efficient material + shape + size combo for thermal comfort in a given region
- Extensible to other climatic regions

## Key challenges
Correct thermal simulation setup (conduction/convection/radiation, thermal mass), model usability, validation against real data.

## Why this difficulty
A focused simulation/modeling task; the difficulty is thermal-physics correctness and validation rather than software breadth.
