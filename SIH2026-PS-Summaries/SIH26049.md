# SIH26049 — Hardware Modifications for Electronics Reliability in Ladakh's High-Altitude Conditions (DRDO)

- **Organization:** DRDO
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 7.5/10

## What the PS is about
Ladakh (-35 to 40 °C, low pressure, thin air, high UV/cosmic radiation, thermal cycling) degrades electronics: poor convective cooling, insulation breakdown/arcing, battery capacity loss, solder-joint cracking, semiconductor degradation, antenna icing. Example: drones that fly 1 h at sea level manage only 20–25 min in Ladakh.

## What to build
Design modifications/methodologies that improve reliability, efficiency and lifespan of electrical/electronic equipment in HAA/SHAA:
- Thermal management solutions compensating for thin-air cooling loss
- Insulation/arcing mitigation for low dielectric strength air
- Battery thermal conditioning / chemistry choices for extreme cold
- Conformal protection against thermal cycling, UV/cosmic-radiation hardening approaches
- Communication-link and antenna-icing mitigations; validated via modeling + testing

## Key challenges
Access to representative environmental testing (thermal chambers, altitude simulation), physics depth across thermal/electrical/material domains.

## Why this difficulty
Environment-hardening engineering is legit but testable at prototype level with clever setups; breadth of failure modes makes it demanding.
