# SIH26071 — AI/ML Integrated Heavy Rainfall Early Warning & Inundation Prediction System

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Disaster Management
- **Difficulty Score:** 7.5/10

## What the PS is about
Heavy-rainfall disasters need integrated prediction combining satellite, radar, observational weather and NWP model data — currently used in silos.

## What to build
An AI/ML system that:
- Fuses multi-source inputs (satellite, Doppler radar, weather observations, NWP) to predict heavy rainfall
- Provides early warning with inundation prediction (where water will accumulate)
- Delivers actionable warnings for authorities

## Key challenges
Multi-modal fusion of heterogeneous meteorological streams, inundation mapping needing terrain data, validation.

## Why this difficulty
Strong meteorology + ML fusion problem with good public data (IMD, MOSDAC); heavy but feasible.
