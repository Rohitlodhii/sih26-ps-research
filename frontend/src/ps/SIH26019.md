# SIH26019 — National Digital Platform for Research, Policy Innovation & Evidence-Based Land Governance

- **Organization:** Ministry of Rural Development (DoLR)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 5/10

## What the PS is about
India's land governance ecosystem lacks a national knowledge hub — datasets, research, policy documents, GIS layers and case studies remain siloed and underused for evidence-based policymaking.

## What to build
An AI-enabled national research & policy platform that:
- Hosts a centralized repository of research papers, policy docs, datasets, legal documents, case studies
- AI-powered search & recommendation engine
- Collaborative workspaces for researchers, policymakers, institutions
- GIS visualization of land use, climate vulnerability, policy impacts
- Policy simulation modules; analytics/decision-support tools; AI-assisted trend analysis and literature synthesis
- Innovation portal (hackathons, grants, pilots); role-based access; APIs to government platforms

## Key challenges
Scope breadth (portal + search + GIS + simulation), but each piece uses mainstream tech.

## Why this difficulty
Ambitious feature list but nothing technically exotic — mostly full-stack + search + visualization. Simulation modules are the only stretch item.
