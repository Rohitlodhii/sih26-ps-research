# SIH26075 — Capacity Connect: Digital Capacity-Building & Learning Management Portal (MoES)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Education
- **Difficulty Score:** 4/10

## What the PS is about
Organizational training (MoES institutions) is managed manually — no central system for courses, assessments, trainers or competency tracking.

## What to build
An LMS portal with three roles:
- **Trainee:** profiles, course enrollment, learning resources, MCQ assessments, feedback
- **Trainer:** questionnaires with deadlines, participation/performance monitoring, lecture/material library
- **Admin:** approvals, role management, dashboards (courses, enrollments, certifications), notifications/announcements
- Competency mapping to match trainers to subjects; secure, responsive, cross-device

## Key challenges
None beyond thorough LMS feature coverage.

## Why this difficulty
Standard CRUD/LMS build — very approachable.
