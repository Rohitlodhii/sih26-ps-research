# SIH26046 — AIIA Clinical Trials Dashboard: GCP-Compliant CTMS for Ayurveda Research

- **Organization:** Ministry of Ayush (All India Institute of Ayurveda)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** MedTech / BioTech / HealthTech
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
AIIA runs multi-centre Ayurveda clinical trials and hosts the national pharmacovigilance centre (NPvCC), but tracks studies in spreadsheets — no real-time, auditable view, causing missed timelines and compliance risk under CTRI/GCP-ASU/NDCT Rules 2019.

## What to build
A cloud-based CTMS + monitoring dashboard:
- Full study lifecycle tracking: protocol/IEC approval, CTRI registration, site activation, enrolment vs target, deviations, milestones, close-out
- Real-time KPIs with configurable alerts; role-based access (PI, coordinator, monitor, EC, pharmacovigilance, regulator read-only)
- Integrated pharmacovigilance: AE/SAE capture and routing against regulatory timelines, MedDRA/WHODrug coding, safety signals to DSMB
- CDISC (CDASH/SDTM/ADaM) data models, HL7 FHIR R4 / ABDM interoperability with EDC and hospital systems
- ALCOA+ data integrity, immutable audit trail, informed-consent management, DPDP Act 2023 compliance, ISO 27001 / CERT-In-secured hosting, submission-ready exports

## Key challenges
Compliance density (GCP, CDISC, FHIR, DPDP), audit-grade architecture — the tech is known but the correctness bar is high.

## Why this difficulty
Enterprise clinical-systems engineering; a prototype is buildable, but doing the standards/interop parts credibly is serious work.
