# SIH26031 — AI-Based Onion Quality Assessment & Grading App

- **Organization:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 4.5/10

## What the PS is about
Onion grading at procurement centres is subjective and varies across centres, causing disputes and inconsistency between farmers and agencies.

## What to build
An AI mobile app that:
- Uses image processing/computer vision on onion photos to assess quality
- Identifies damaged, rotten, sprouted, undersized onions
- Estimates Grade A and URS (Unsorted/Reject) percentages
- Generates an instant digital quality report; removes human bias

## Key challenges
Getting a labeled onion image dataset and handling field lighting/conditions; model accuracy for a handful of defect classes.

## Why this difficulty
A focused image-classification app — standard CV pipeline, small scope. One of the most approachable PS in the list.
