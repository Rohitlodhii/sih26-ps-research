# SIH26072 — AI/ML Nowcasting of Thunderstorms & Lightning (Multi-Radar, Satellite, Lightning & Model Data)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Disaster Management
- **Difficulty Score:** 7.5/10

## What the PS is about
Thunderstorms and lightning kill hundreds yearly in India; short-horizon (0–2 h) nowcasting from multiple radars, satellite, lightning networks and models is needed for actionable warnings.

## What to build
An AI/ML nowcasting system that:
- Ingests atmospheric observations: multiple Doppler radars, satellite imagery, lightning detection data, NWP model fields
- Predicts thunderstorm/lightning initiation and movement in the nowcast window
- Outputs warnings with location specificity

## Key challenges
Radar data processing (reflectivity/velocity), storm-cell tracking, high class imbalance, strict latency.

## Why this difficulty
Similar family to 26071/26084 with a narrower scope; strong public datasets and known baselines (e.g., precipitation nowcasting deep nets) make it demanding but doable.
