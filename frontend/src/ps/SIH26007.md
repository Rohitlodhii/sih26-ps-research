# SIH26007 — Safe & Efficient Operation of Mine Vehicles in Fog / Low Visibility (Open Cast Iron Ore Mines)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Steel (NMDC)
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 8.5/10

## What the PS is about
NMDC's Bailadila mines face 3–5 m visibility in dense monsoon fog, forcing dumpers to slow or stop — causing accidents risk, longer haul cycles and production losses in a 100 MTPA-scale operation.

## What to build
A system enabling safe vehicle movement in low visibility via:
- Computer vision + thermal imaging, LiDAR/radar sensing for obstacle detection
- GPS/DGPS vehicle tracking; V2V and V2I communication
- Driver assistance / collision avoidance and guidance
- IoT monitoring + centralized command-and-control platform; optional digital twin
- Real-time decision support for operators and control rooms

## Key challenges
Sensor fusion in dust/fog/rain, safety-critical reliability on massive haul trucks, integration with existing fleet and mine infrastructure.

## Why this difficulty
Safety-critical automotive-grade perception hardware in extreme conditions — high engineering bar, real-world testing hard to arrange for students.
