# SIH26100 — AI-Powered Integrated Bid Compliance Verification Platform for GeM Procurement

- **Organization:** Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 6/10

## What the PS is about
GeM procurement officers manually verify bidder compliance (Udyam/MSME, GST, PAN/IT, Make in India, EPFO/ESIC, Startup India, NSIC, OEM authorization, DigiLocker, blacklisting) across many portals — slow, error-prone tender evaluation.

## What to build
An AI verification platform that:
- Integrates with government portals/databases to auto-retrieve and verify bidder registrations and compliance status
- Uses an AI Verification Engine on submitted documents + portal data to find missing/inconsistent info and validate tender-specific eligibility
- Produces a Compliance Score, Risk Level, document-verification status, pending items and AI recommendations on a dashboard
- Keeps the final qualification decision with the procurement officer; full audit trail
- Target impact: 60–80% reduction in verification effort

## Key challenges
Portal integration realism (sandboxing), document extraction accuracy, rule encoding for varied tender terms.

## Why this difficulty
Document AI + rules engine + dashboard; the integration assumptions are the main risk, otherwise conventional tech.
