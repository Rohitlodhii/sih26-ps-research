# SIH26026 — Quadruped/Handheld Device for Real-Time Detection of Narcotics & Explosives (Indian Railways)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Railways (RPF)
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Blockchain & Cybersecurity
- **Difficulty Score:** 9.5/10

## What the PS is about
~15–20% of drug trafficking routes through railways; RPF recovered ₹300 Cr of narcotics in 2025, and IED incidents (RDX on tracks) expose infrastructure vulnerability. Railways has no dedicated mobile detection system for field screening.

## What to build
Two systems:
1. **AI-enabled quadruped robot** — autonomous/semi-autonomous patrol with LiDAR mapping, sensor fusion, thermal+optical imaging, underframe coach inspection, yard surveillance, night patrolling, facial recognition, GPS-denied navigation, obstacle avoidance, real-time transmission to control centres
2. **Handheld rugged detector** — non-intrusive narcotics/explosives screening for personnel, offline+online, encrypted logging, GPS tagging, multilingual UI, wireless alerts
- Must detect heroin, cocaine, meth, cannabis, opium derivatives; and RDX, TNT, PETN, TATP, C4, ammonium nitrate, gunpowder etc.

## Key challenges
Detecting trace narcotics/explosives requires specialized sensing (ion mobility / spectroscopy-grade hardware) that is genuinely hard to build; plus a legged robot with autonomous navigation in railway environments.

## Why this difficulty
Two frontier hardware problems (chemical detection sensors + quadruped robotics) combined. One of the toughest PS in the list; realistically only partially achievable as a student prototype.
