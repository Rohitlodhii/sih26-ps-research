# SIH26086 — Hyperlocal Monsoon Onset & Break Prediction System (Block/Village Scale)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Difficulty Score:** 8/10

## What the PS is about
False monsoon onsets followed by break phases destroy crops when farmers sow too early. Subdivision-level forecasts lack block/panchayat granularity for sowing decisions.

## What to build
A hybrid framework giving a 7–30-day probabilistic monsoon outlook at block/panchayat scale:
- Ingests climate teleconnections (ENSO, IOD, MJO) plus regional data; ML-downscales to local rainfall anomalies, onset thresholds and active/break durations
- Color-coded risk maps: probability of onset, dry spells, heavy rain 1–4 weeks ahead
- Expert system converting probabilities into crop-specific advisories (delay sowing, irrigation prep, crop choice)
- Mobile/SMS/WhatsApp advisories in regional languages for farmers and extension officers

## Key challenges
Bridging global climate indices to village-scale outcomes — a hard downscaling/statistical learning problem with sparse ground truth.

## Why this difficulty
Seasonal-to-subseasonal prediction is a research frontier; the advisory/UX layers are easy, the predictive core is hard.
