# SIH26015 — Geospatial Techniques for Visualization & Analysis of Geo-Coded Images to Enhance Watershed Development Outcomes

- **Organization:** Ministry of Rural Development (DoLR)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Difficulty Score:** 6/10

## What the PS is about
Watershed programs lack scientific monitoring: geo-tagged field photos are collected but only used for documentation, never integrated with satellite data (e.g., the 30 m resolution SRISHTI-DRISHTI platform) for spatial analysis of land degradation, drainage, vegetation change and conservation-structure impact.

## What to build
A systematic framework/toolkit that:
- Visualizes and interprets geo-coded field images alongside satellite-derived thematic layers and watershed boundaries
- Supports thematic mapping and spatial analysis (land degradation, water structures, vegetation/drainage change)
- Produces evidence-based planning/monitoring insights for watershed interventions in a cost-effective, scalable way

## Key challenges
Methodology design for fusing ground photos with RS data; mostly analysis rather than product building.

## Why this difficulty
Research/analysis-oriented with accessible GIS/RS tools (QGIS, Google Earth Engine style); limited software engineering risk, but requires geospatial analysis skill and clear deliverable framing.
