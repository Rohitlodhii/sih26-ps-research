# SIH26054 — AI-Enabled Real-Time Digital Twin for Aero Piston Engines of MALE UAVs (DRDO)

- **Organization:** DRDO
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Robotics and Drones
- **Difficulty Score:** 8.5/10

## What the PS is about
MALE UAV piston engines are monitored reactively with threshold alarms — no remaining-useful-life (RUL) estimation, degradation trending or mission-wise behavior simulation. Failures mean lost missions and unsafe recoveries.

## What to build
A modular digital twin framework for aero piston engines:
- Real-time virtual engine synchronized via CAN bus/Socket CAN and ECU/FADEC interfaces; edge + local/cloud analytics
- Health monitoring across RPM, CHT, EGT, oil pressure/temp, fuel flow, vibration, battery/alternator, injection timing → health indices
- Predictive diagnostics: misfires, injector anomalies, cooling degradation, lubrication issues, sensor drift, combustion instability, overheating and vibration trends; RUL estimation
- Physics-informed + AI/ML anomaly detection; mission-profile simulation and post-flight replay
- Operator/maintenance dashboards/HMI suitable for future defence GCS integration

## Key challenges
Engine physics modeling, telemetry simulation/acquisition, fusing physics + ML for credible RUL, real-time architecture.

## Why this difficulty
Digital-twin + predictive-maintenance on a specialized engine with little public data — needs strong simulation, ML and systems skills.
