# SIH26088 — Multilingual Cooperative Governance & Legal Assistance Chatbot

- **Organization:** Ministry of Cooperation
- **Category:** Hardware (listed) | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Difficulty Score:** 4.5/10

## What the PS is about
Cooperative members, farmers and rural stakeholders lack accessible guidance on cooperative laws, schemes (including PMFBY), financial literacy and grievance redressal due to language barriers.

## What to build
An AI-powered multilingual chatbot providing:
- Guidance on cooperative laws/by-laws and Ministry of Cooperation schemes
- PMFBY and agricultural support guidance; financial-literacy assistance
- Cooperative grievance redressal support
- Voice-enabled interaction (STT/TTS) for rural users on mobile/web

## Key challenges
Grounded answers on legal/scheme content (RAG over official documents), Indian-language voice.

## Why this difficulty
Familiar GenAI chatbot scope; content curation and vernacular voice quality are the main effort.
