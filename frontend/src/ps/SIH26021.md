# SIH26021 — Honey Chain: Blockchain-based Honey Traceability & Smart Beekeeping

- **Organization:** Ministry of MSME (KVIC Honey Mission)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
Rural beekeepers under KVIC's Honey Mission face counterfeit honey, low consumer trust, weak market linkage and no traceability. Buyers can't verify authenticity.

## What to build
"Honey Chain" — an integrated blockchain + AI + IoT ecosystem:
- Blockchain-based batch tracking from hive to jar with QR-code consumer verification
- IoT hive monitoring (environment sensors) feeding the chain
- AI analytics for disease detection, colony health tracking, productivity prediction
- Secure batch records and a scalable deployment framework for rural beekeeping clusters

## Key challenges
Meaningful blockchain design (not gimmicky), IoT-to-chain integration, AI models on limited beekeeping data.

## Why this difficulty
Three tech stacks combined (blockchain, IoT, AI) plus field deployment; each is individually manageable but integration and credibility of the AI piece take work.
