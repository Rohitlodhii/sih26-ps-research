# SIH26036 — Online Verification System for Weighing & Measuring Instruments

- **Organization:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Miscellaneous
- **Difficulty Score:** 4/10

## What the PS is about
Legal Metrology officers (LMOs) and Government Approved Test Centres verify/stamp weighing instruments via manual applications, paper records and isolated systems — slow, opaque, with no centralized verification history.

## What to build
A web + mobile platform for the full verification lifecycle:
- Stakeholder registration (users, LMOs, GATCs)
- Online application, scheduling and allocation of verification/re-verification
- Digital field recording of inspection observations
- QR-coded digital verification certificates with authentication
- Validity tracking, expiry alerts/reminders, pendency dashboards, search/retrieval, document uploads, role-based secure login

## Key challenges
None technically beyond solid workflow engineering and QR certificate security.

## Why this difficulty
Classic e-governance workflow application — very buildable by any competent full-stack team.
