# SIH26022 — Solar-Powered Smart Drying & Packaging System for Home-Based Agarbatti Artisans

- **Organization:** Ministry of MSME
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Difficulty Score:** 6/10

## What the PS is about
Rural women making agarbatti (incense sticks) at home depend on weather for drying, causing uneven drying, fragrance loss, fungal growth and breakage — hurting quality and income.

## What to build
A compact, low-cost, solar-powered smart drying chamber plus a simple packaging solution:
- Controlled drying mechanism with temperature/humidity sensors and uniform airflow in a hygienic enclosed chamber
- Battery backup for all-weather use; portable, easy to operate
- Optional AI/IoT monitoring and alerts
- Low-cost moisture-resistant, fragrance-preserving packaging with a compact sealing mechanism suitable for SHGs; eco-friendly options

## Key challenges
Solar thermal design at low cost, humidity/airflow control, fragrance preservation, and simple mechanics for home users.

## Why this difficulty
Well-scoped consumer-scale hardware; the engineering is in thermal control and cost optimization rather than cutting-edge tech.
