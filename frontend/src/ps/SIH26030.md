# SIH26030 — Automated Cable Specimen Preparation System (IS 10810 / IS 7098 Compliance)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 6.5/10

## What the PS is about
Cable testing (conductor resistance, insulation/sheath thickness, flame retardance) requires precisely prepared specimens. Prep is manual: cutting, straightening, slicing, dumbbell shaping — slow, inconsistent, error-prone.

## What to build
An automated specimen-prep machine that:
- Feeds and clamps cable via motor-driven rollers with auto-adjust (integrated diameter sensor)
- Precision cutting/stripping module with programmable blade depths for circumferential cuts and linear stripping to IS-specified lengths
- PLC/HMI control with test-method selection, status monitoring, closed-loop feedback
- Automated specimen ejection and waste handling; safety enclosures and interlocks

## Key challenges
Mechanical precision (clean cuts on PVC/XLPE/HDPE), automation of a multi-step physical process, standards-exact dimensions.

## Why this difficulty
Mechatronics problem — motors, blades, sensors, PLC — well-defined but requires solid mechanical design and iteration.
