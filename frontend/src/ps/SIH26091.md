# SIH26091 — AI Hyper-Local Business Advisory & Financial Structuring Assistant for Rural Micro-Entrepreneurs (MoSJE)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (MoSJE)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Difficulty Score:** 5.5/10

## What the PS is about
Rural first-time entrepreneurs under MoSJE concessional-loan schemes (10% margin money + 90% loan; Micro Finance up to ₹1.40L @6.5%, Term Loan to ₹50L @8%) pick businesses on anecdote, not data — leading to stagnation.

## What to build
A multilingual NLP assistant with two modules:
1. **Hyper-local feasibility report** given location + margin capital + business category: market reach (5–10 km consumer base), opportunity niches, SWOT, local threats (supply/seasonality/single-buyer risk), competitor density mapping, and market-value/pricing suggestions from regional purchasing power
2. **Smart financial calculator & scheme router:** auto-computes feasible project cost (margin ÷ 10%), max loan (90%), and auto-selects eligible scheme with repayment roadmap

## Key challenges
Sourcing credible hyper-local demographic/economic data for market analysis; grounded LLM generation.

## Why this difficulty
LLM + rules hybrid with a deterministic financial engine — the calculator part is trivial, the localized market analysis data is the real work.
