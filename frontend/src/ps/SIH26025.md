# SIH26025 — AI-Enabled Low-Cost Real-Time Mine Subsidence Monitoring, Prediction & Early Warning (Underground Coal Mines)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Coal
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 7.5/10

## What the PS is about
Ground subsidence above underground coal mines threatens communities and infrastructure; monitoring today is periodic surveys and post-facto damage assessment with no timely warning.

## What to build
A wireless surface mesh sensor network + AI early-warning platform:
- Low-cost smart nodes (Arduino/ESP32/RPi) with tilt/inclination, vibration, displacement/stretch, crack sensors (optional positioning)
- LoRa/Zigbee/Wi-Fi mesh communication over mine panels
- Continuous detection of abnormal tilt, node-distance change, crack initiation, unusual vibration
- AI/ML anomaly detection and subsidence prediction; severity/progression estimation
- GIS visualization of deformation maps and risk zones; SMS/email/app alerts; offline capability with cloud sync; interactive dashboards

## Key challenges
Low-power mesh networking in field conditions, calibrated sensing of micro-movements, ML with little labeled subsidence data.

## Why this difficulty
Genuine IoT+ML+GIS integration with hardware deployment physics; the PS explicitly wants student-buildable low-cost hardware, which helps, but reliability engineering is real.
