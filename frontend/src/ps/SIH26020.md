# SIH26020 — Innovative Hand-Spinning Equipment for Khadi Artisan Productivity and Income

- **Organization:** Ministry of MSME (Coordination Section)
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Difficulty Score:** 6/10

## What the PS is about
Khadi yarn is hand-spun on manually operated charkhas (e.g., New Model Charkha) by mostly women artisans. Current systems are low-efficiency, physically uncomfortable and produce inconsistent yarn quality, capping artisan income.

## What to build
An improved, lightweight, portable, ergonomic manually operated spinning system:
- Prototype with higher productivity, better ergonomics, reduced drudgery, consistent yarn quality and better aesthetics — while keeping the traditional, sustainable, manual nature of khadi
- Comparative evaluation vs existing charkhas: yarn quality, productivity, effort, portability, weight, manufacturing cost
- Deployment & dissemination framework for field trials and artisan adoption
- Cost-benefit analysis, economic impact assessment, scalability roadmap

## Key challenges
Mechanical design iteration, ergonomic testing with real users, yarn-quality consistency, cost optimization for rural manufacturing.

## Why this difficulty
Focused mechanical-hardware problem with clear evaluation criteria; no software/ML, but physical prototyping and field validation take real effort.
