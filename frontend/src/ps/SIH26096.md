# SIH26096 — Digital Heritage Archive for Dr. B. R. Ambedkar: AI-Powered Institutional Knowledge Platform

- **Organization:** Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (MoSJE)
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Smart Education
- **Difficulty Score:** 5.5/10

## What the PS is about
Ambedkar's writings, speeches, manuscripts and constitutional debates are scattered across libraries and fragmented sources, with no intelligent, multilingual, interactive access for institutions like the Dr. Ambedkar International Centre.

## What to build
A hardware+software heritage archive:
- Interactive touch-screen kiosks and smart displays + centralized archival servers
- AI semantic search and knowledge mapping across the corpus
- OCR digitization of old documents/manuscripts; full-text and summarized access
- Multilingual translation and audio narration; audio-video archive of lectures/documentaries
- Interactive timeline and memorial storytelling modules; AI research assistant answering questions on Ambedkar's works and constitutional ideas
- Secure digital preservation, metadata tagging, archival management

## Key challenges
OCR on historical documents, curated RAG assistant over the corpus, kiosk UX.

## Why this difficulty
Composeable from known tech (OCR + RAG + kiosks); the effort is corpus digitization curation and experience design.
