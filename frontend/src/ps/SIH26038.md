# SIH26038 — Explainable AI for Diabetic Retinopathy Screening in Rural India (MathWorks)

- **Organization:** MathWorks
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** MedTech / BioTech / HealthTech
- **Difficulty Score:** 8.5/10

## What the PS is about
India has 77M+ diabetic adults; DR affects ~18% and is a leading cause of preventable blindness. With ~1 ophthalmologist per 100k rural population, manual screening can't scale — and existing AI is a black box that fails on variable field image quality.

## What to build
A MATLAB-based DR screening pipeline:
1. Image quality assessment/enhancement (CLAHE, illumination normalization) with rejection + recapture feedback for ungradeable images
2. Retinal structure segmentation: optic disc/fovea localization, vessels, microaneurysms, exudates, hemorrhages, neovascularization
3. Severity grading on the International DR scale (0–4) with >90% sensitivity and >85% specificity for referable DR
4. Explainability: Grad-CAM maps, lesion-level evidence, calibrated confidence, annotated reports enabling <30s ophthalmologist validation
5. Simulink workflow model of the telemedicine pipeline (bandwidth, throughput, review capacity) for district programs serving 100k+ patients/year

## Key challenges
Meeting hard clinical accuracy targets, sub-pixel lesion detection, credible explainability, and benchmark validation.

## Why this difficulty
Medical-imaging AI with explicit quantitative clinical thresholds — among the most demanding software PS, though datasets (APTOS, EyePACS, IDRiD) are public.
