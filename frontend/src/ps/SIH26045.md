# SIH26045 — IP-SAKTI Sahayak: RAG-Based AI Assistant for Ayurveda IP & Regulatory Guidance

- **Organization:** Ministry of Ayush
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** MedTech / BioTech / HealthTech
- **Difficulty Score:** 6.5/10

## What the PS is about
AYUSH practitioners, startups and MSMEs must navigate patents, GI, trademarks, biodiversity (ABS) duties and drug regulatory categories (classical vs proprietary medicine vs new drug vs nutraceutical vs cosmetic) — across Indian and international regimes (TRIPS, Nagoya, WIPO GRATK). No plain-language guidance tool exists.

## What to build
A multilingual, source-cited RAG assistant that:
- Answers IPR/regulatory questions grounded in a curated, version-tracked corpus (statutes, rules, treaties, registries, case law) with mandatory citations and confidence indicators
- Jurisdiction toggle (India vs international) with visibly separate answer sets
- Formulation classification flow (classical/proprietary/new drug/phytopharmaceutical/Aahar/cosmetic) determining the relevant IP/ABS posture
- ABS-compliance helper and TKDL/prior-art pointer; routing to correct registries and forms
- Clear "information, not legal advice" stance; no fabricated authorities

## Key challenges
Curating a legally accurate corpus, hallucination control, correct statutory citations, multi-regime reasoning.

## Why this difficulty
RAG is now standard, but legal-domain precision and corpus curation demand real diligence; no hardware, moderate ML.
