# SIH26032 — Farmer Procurement Queue Management & Slot Booking Platform

- **Organization:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 3.5/10

## What the PS is about
Farmers face long waits, no information on procurement schedules, and uncertainty about procurement status at government procurement centres.

## What to build
A platform that:
- Handles farmer registration and slot booking
- Provides real-time queue management at centres
- Sends SMS/app notifications
- Tracks procurement and payment status
- Reduces congestion and waiting time

## Key challenges
Almost none technically — standard web/mobile CRUD with notifications. Main work is UX for low-literacy users and SMS integration.

## Why this difficulty
The most beginner-friendly PS of the set: a straightforward booking + queue + notification system.
