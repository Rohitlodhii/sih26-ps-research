# SIH26062 — Integrated Polar Expedition Logistics & Asset Management System

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 4.5/10

## What the PS is about
Polar expeditions involve complex planning — cargo, inventory, personnel, emergencies — managed today through fragmented processes.

## What to build
A centralized digital platform covering:
- Expedition planning workflows
- Cargo tracking and inventory management
- Personnel movement records
- Emergency response coordination

## Key challenges
Workflow design breadth only; no hard AI/hardware.

## Why this difficulty
A well-bounded management-information system — very buildable by a standard full-stack team.
