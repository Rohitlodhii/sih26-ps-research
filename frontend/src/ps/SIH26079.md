# SIH26079 — AI-Based Forecast Bust Detection for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
Medium-range forecasts (Day 1–10) sometimes fail badly ("forecast busts") during monsoon depressions, heat waves and break/active phases, misleading operational decisions.

## What to build
An AI/ML system that:
- Compares current NWP forecast patterns against historical forecast-error behavior
- Produces region-wise confidence maps for Day 1–10, forecast-bust probabilities, and error-prone area detection
- Provides explainable outputs (key meteorological reasons for low confidence)
- Ships a prototype dashboard/API for operational use

## Key challenges
Constructing historical error archives, learning when models fail, explainability.

## Why this difficulty
A well-defined verification/ML problem with IMD/NCEP data available; moderate but needs meteorological insight.
