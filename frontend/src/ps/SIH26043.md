# SIH26043 — Platform to Crowdsource Societal Challenges & Route Them to Universities and Industry (Jharkhand)

- **Organization:** Government of Jharkhand
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Education
- **Difficulty Score:** 4.5/10

## What the PS is about
Citizens identify local problems (education, health, water, sanitation, livelihoods) but there's no mechanism to route them to universities/industry for solutioning; collaboration today is fragmented and ad hoc.

## What to build
A societal innovation collaboration portal:
- Citizen/organization/government submission of challenges with photos, video, location, documents (web + mobile)
- AI classification/prioritization/dedup and routing to universities by expertise
- University workflow: review, form teams, prepare proposals
- Industry/CSR/startup collaboration for mentorship, funding, prototyping
- Workflow management: review, allocation, milestones, validation; analytics dashboards for government

## Key challenges
Workflow breadth and matching logic; AI routing can be simple NLP classification.

## Why this difficulty
A multi-role web platform with modest AI — well within a standard full-stack team's reach.
