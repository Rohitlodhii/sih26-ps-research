# SIH26057 — AI-Powered Underwater Marine Debris & Anomaly Detection from Side-Scan Sonar Imagery (MoES)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Disaster Management
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
Ghost nets (abandoned fishing gear) kill marine life and destroy reefs. Side-scan sonar maps the seafloor, but manual inspection of sonar logs is slow and error-prone — debris blends into rocks, sand ripples and acoustic shadows.

## What to build
An end-to-end CV pipeline for side-scan sonar imagery:
- Detection/segmentation model (YOLO / Faster R-CNN / U-Net) bounding man-made objects: shipwrecks, pipes, cylinders, entangled nets
- Confidence scoring + noise filtering to suppress false positives from shadows/rock clusters
- Geotagging engine parsing sonar metadata (ping headers/coordinates) into structured JSON/CSV reports (location, dimensions, classification)
- UI dashboard: upload sonar logs, view detections overlaid on the map, download reports; edge-device-friendly efficiency

## Key challenges
Acoustic imagery is brutal for CV (speckle noise, shadows, dropouts, varying resolution) and labeled sonar datasets are scarce.

## Why this difficulty
Solid object-detection work, but the data domain (sonar) is unusual and data acquisition/augmentation is the real hurdle.
