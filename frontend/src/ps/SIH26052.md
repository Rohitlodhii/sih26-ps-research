# SIH26052 — AI/ML-Enabled Adaptive Noise Cancellation for Defence Communications (DRDO)

- **Organization:** DRDO
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Miscellaneous
- **Difficulty Score:** 8.5/10

## What the PS is about
Gunshots, artillery, rotor noise and sirens wreck speech comms; classical DSP (spectral subtraction, Wiener, LMS-ANC) fails on non-stationary, impulsive noise. Deep-learning ANC now outperforms them and edge AI hardware makes real-time deployment feasible.

## What to build
A hybrid AI + adaptive-filtering ANC pipeline:
- Dataset pipeline mixing clean speech with defence noise (gunfire, drones, engines, wind) at varying SNRs; augmentation (reverb, clipping)
- Deep model on STFT spectrograms or raw waveform, full-band + sub-band, complex-domain for phase preservation; losses like SI-SNR and perceptual loss
- Real-time inference on embedded hardware (e.g., Jetson Orin) with quantization/pruning/ONNX-TensorRT optimization
- Mic + reference-mic headset prototype demonstrating live cancellation; residual LMS stage optional
- Targets: SNR > 15 dB, STOI > 0.85, PESQ > 2.5 at low latency

## Key challenges
Meeting the stated metrics on impulse noise, real-time embedded optimization, dataset construction.

## Why this difficulty
Serious audio deep learning + embedded deployment; models exist in literature, but hitting targets on defence noise at the edge is hard.
