# SIH26060 — Digital Platform for Efficient Remote Management of Indian Antarctic Research Stations (Maitri & Bharati)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
India's Antarctic stations (Maitri, Bharati) are extremely remote; infrastructure, energy, logistics and environmental monitoring need a unified digital-twin framework for efficient remote management from India.

## What to build
A Digital Twin framework integrating:
- Infrastructure monitoring (buildings, systems)
- Energy management (power generation/consumption, likely diesel + renewables)
- Logistics tracking (supplies, inventory, movement)
- Environmental monitoring (weather, station footprint)
- Real-time dashboard/visualization mirroring station state for remote decision-making

## Key challenges
Very short PS description — scope must be inferred; integrating simulated sensor feeds into a coherent twin, communication-constrained data sync assumptions.

## Why this difficulty
Digital twin breadth across four domains; the technical core (IoT data + dashboards + models) is standard, but making the twin genuinely useful with limited real data is the challenge.
