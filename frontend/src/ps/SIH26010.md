# SIH26010 — Survey/Resurvey of Rural Agricultural Land in India

- **Organization:** Ministry of Rural Development (DoLR)
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
Rural land surveys still rely on decades-old chain-and-tape measurements, causing boundary errors, encroachments, record-map mismatches and a huge share of civil litigation.

## What to build
A technology-driven survey/resurvey pipeline:
- Drone-based aerial surveys, RTK/DGPS, GNSS/CORS for accurate parcel mapping
- GIS-enabled cadastral mapping and geo-referenced parcel IDs
- A unified digital land information system integrating Record of Rights, mutation records, registration databases, survey maps, ownership history and geo-coordinates — with real-time updating/verification

## Key challenges
Survey-grade accuracy (RTK/drones), handling massive field operations, integrating legacy record systems across states.

## Why this difficulty
Hardware accuracy requirements and large-scale systems integration; conceptually clear but engineering-heavy and field-dependent.
