# SIH26011 — 3D ULPIN Generation and Vertical Property Mapping System

- **Organization:** Ministry of Rural Development (DoLR)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 8/10

## What the PS is about
2D land records can't represent ownership of multi-storey apartments, underground infrastructure, air rights, parking or utility networks. India needs a 3D cadastre.

## What to build
A 3D ULPIN (Unique Land Parcel Identification Number) and vertical property mapping system that:
- Creates unique spatial identities for surface parcels, multi-storey apartments, and underground assets
- Integrates drone imagery, LiDAR/3D point clouds, GIS parcel layers, building floor plans, GNSS/CORS coordinates, DEM/DSM
- Uses AI/ML for automated building extraction, floor segmentation, vertical parcel delineation, topology validation
- Supports volumetric cadastre and standardized 3D ULPINs

## Key challenges
3D point-cloud processing, volumetric parcel modeling (a niche, emerging research area), interoperable 3D cadastral standards.

## Why this difficulty
Research-heavy 3D geospatial problem with little off-the-shelf tooling; strong signal-processing + geometry skills needed.
