# SIH26074 — Downscaling Weather Forecasts from Block to Panchayat Level for Agro-Meteorological Advisories

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
Weather forecasts exist at block level (~10 km grids), but farmers need panchayat-level (village) granularity for sowing, irrigation and spraying decisions.

## What to build
A downscaling system that:
- Infers high-resolution panchayat-level weather data/plots from low-resolution block-level forecasts and variables
- Produces visualizations/data products feeding agro-meteorological advisory services

## Key challenges
Statistical/dynamical downscaling methodology, validation ground truth at village scale, terrain-aware corrections.

## Why this difficulty
A classic ML super-resolution/downscaling problem; data is available but proving genuine village-level skill is the hard part.
