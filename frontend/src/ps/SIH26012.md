# SIH26012 — AI-Based Automated Urban Parcel Mapping & Cadastral Feature Extraction from Drone Imagery

- **Organization:** Ministry of Rural Development (DoLR)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 7/10

## What the PS is about
Urban cadastral maps are still made by manually interpreting drone imagery and field ground-truthing — slow and error-prone, especially in dense, irregular urban layouts.

## What to build
An AI/GeoAI platform that automatically:
- Extracts parcel boundaries and building footprints from high-res drone/orthorectified imagery, DSM/DTM, GIS layers and GNSS survey data
- Detects roads, pathways and access corridors; classifies urban land use
- Runs image segmentation + deep-learning object detection to generate parcel polygons
- Flags overlapping/inconsistent geometries; automated topology generation and validation
- Provides a Web-GIS visualization/editing dashboard producing GIS-ready cadastral outputs

## Key challenges
Training accurate segmentation models on urban imagery (dataset availability), handling irregular geometries and occlusions.

## Why this difficulty
Core computer-vision segmentation work with real data needs; well-defined but model-accuracy-dependent.
