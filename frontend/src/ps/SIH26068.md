# SIH26068 — WeatherGPT: Conversational AI for Weather Forecasting, Alerts & Climate Information

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Disaster Management
- **Difficulty Score:** 5/10

## What the PS is about
Weather info is scattered across portals and bulletins; citizens, farmers and disaster managers need one conversational, multilingual, voice-enabled weather assistant.

## What to build
An LLM-powered chatbot platform that:
- Retrieves real-time weather info and forecasts via natural language
- Integrates NWP model outputs (GFS/WRF) and meteorological APIs/databases
- Disseminates extreme-weather alerts and early warnings
- Location-based forecasting, climate trend analysis, multilingual support, voice interaction for rural users
- Stack suggestions: FastAPI/Node, MQTT/WIS2.0/WebSocket, LLMs, GIS, PostgreSQL/MongoDB, Docker/K8s

## Key challenges
Reliable LLM grounding on live weather APIs, latency, multilingual voice.

## Why this difficulty
Now-familiar RAG/LLM-agent territory with weather APIs; achievable and well-liked, but crowded competition.
