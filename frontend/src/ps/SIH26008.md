# SIH26008 — Intelligent Monitoring & Prediction of Conveyor Belt Joint Rupture and Damage (Iron Ore Mining)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Steel (NMDC)
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Smart Automation
- **Difficulty Score:** 7.5/10

## What the PS is about
Conveyor belts are the backbone of ore transport in iron ore mines; belt joints fail suddenly from tension, misalignment, wear and overload, causing downtime, safety risk and repair cost. Maintenance today is reactive/schedule-based.

## What to build
An intelligent conveyor belt health monitoring & predictive maintenance system:
- IoT sensors: vibration, temperature, belt tracking, acoustic, load, speed, tension
- AI vision + thermal cameras detecting cracks, tears, overheating, misalignment
- Drone/camera-based inspection
- Digital twin of the conveyor system
- Integration with existing SCADA/PLC systems
- AI/ML predictive analytics for early failure warnings

## Key challenges
Harsh-environment sensor deployment, vision models for defect detection, SCADA/PLC integration, digital twin fidelity.

## Why this difficulty
Solid industrial-IoT scope; the hardware + legacy-system integration and defect-vision accuracy push it above average.
