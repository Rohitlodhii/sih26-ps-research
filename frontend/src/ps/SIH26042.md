# SIH26042 — AI Vernacular Pedagogy & Real-Time Translation Tool for Mother-Tongue Primary Education (Jharkhand)

- **Organization:** Government of Jharkhand
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Education
- **Difficulty Score:** 8.5/10

## What the PS is about
Jharkhand's PALASH MTB-MLE programme is bottlenecked: most teachers are Hindi-medium and can't speak Ho, Mundari or Santhali — languages with minimal digital NLP resources — so 5,000+ tribal-area schools teach in a language children don't understand.

## What to build
An AI translation & curriculum-generation suite:
- NLP engine translating Hindi FLN curriculum content (lesson scripts, activity instructions, assessments) into text + synthesized audio in tribal languages
- Real-time voice-to-voice translation (teacher speaks Hindi, students hear tribal language) with ≤3s latency
- Auto-generated bilingual worksheets and visual flashcards aligned to NIPUN Bharat outcomes
- Fully offline on low-end tablets (2 GB RAM, Android 9+) after initial sync

## Key challenges
Extremely low-resource languages — little to no parallel corpora, no TTS, on-device inference on weak hardware. This is frontier low-resource NLP.

## Why this difficulty
Even one tribal language at prototype stage requires creative data work (crowdsourcing, fine-tuning multilingual models); the latency + offline constraints add more.
