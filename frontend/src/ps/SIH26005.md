# SIH26005 — Solar-Powered Smart Mini Cold Storage System for Fresh Vegetables in NER

- **Organization:** Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MDoNER)
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Difficulty Score:** 6.5/10

## What the PS is about
NER farmers lose large portions of fresh vegetables post-harvest due to missing cold-chain infrastructure, unreliable power and long transport times to markets.

## What to build
A decentralized, village-level solar-powered mini cold storage unit that:
- Preserves vegetables (tomato, cabbage, leafy greens, chilli, etc.) under controlled temperature/humidity
- Runs on solar with battery backup for off-grid operation
- Smart monitoring: temperature/humidity fluctuation, power-failure alerts, storage status
- Compact, modular, weather-resistant for hilly terrain
- Low maintenance, easy operation for farmer cooperatives; digital display/monitoring interface

## Key challenges
Thermal/cooling engineering under solar power constraints, insulation design, cost optimization, and real-world durability.

## Why this difficulty
Well-scoped single hardware product, but getting reliable cooling on a solar+battery budget at low cost requires real engineering iteration.
