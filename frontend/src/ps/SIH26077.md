# SIH26077 — AI-Driven Hyper-Local Early Warning System for Severe Weather Nowcasting (2–6 h Lead)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Disaster Management
- **Difficulty Score:** 8.5/10

## What the PS is about
Cloudbursts, severe thunderstorms and flash floods intensify too fast for physics-based NWP. India needs real-time hyper-local nowcasting 2–6 hours before impact.

## What to build
A multi-task spatiotemporal deep-learning engine that:
- Tracks integrated water vapor (IWV) accumulation (satellite WV channels from INSAT-3D/3DR via MOSDAC) to spot moisture pools triggering cloudbursts
- Assesses instability (CAPE/CIN), lift (low-level convergence, wind shear) and cloud-top cooling rates
- Uses a shared neural backbone with task branches producing probability risk maps for thunderstorms, cloudbursts and flash floods simultaneously
- Overlays precipitation probabilities on high-resolution DEM to predict flash-flood channels
- Trained on IMDAA reanalysis + satellite data; bypasses NWP computational latency

## Key challenges
Multi-task learning on massive spatiotemporal grids, rare-event labels, physically grounded validation.

## Why this difficulty
Cutting-edge nowcasting ML; well-detailed PS (datasets named) but the modeling bar is research-level.
