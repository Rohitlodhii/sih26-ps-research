# SIH26082 — Air Pollution–Weather Coupled Forecasting System (Delhi NCR, 72-h AQI)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Clean & Green Technology
- **Difficulty Score:** 8.5/10

## What the PS is about
AQI forecasting treats weather and pollution separately, but in Delhi NCR they feed back on each other: inversion layers trap PM2.5, and dense aerosols alter radiation, winds and boundary-layer height. uncoupled models are inaccurate.

## What to build
A high-resolution coupled forecasting system that:
- Uses WRF-Chem (or similar open coupled framework) with two-way feedback between meteorology (temperature, wind, PBL height) and chemistry (PM2.5, PM10, O3, NOx)
- Models how inversions trap stubble-burning plumes and how trapped pollutants alter local weather
- Delivers a real-time dashboard with 72-h AQI forecasts, inversion-strength tracking, and stubble-plume dispersion predictions

## Key challenges
WRF-Chem setup/compute cost, emission inventories, coupling validation — heavy atmospheric-science modeling.

## Why this difficulty
Running and calibrating coupled chemistry-climate models is expert territory; a strong, well-resourced entry could differentiate via ML emulation of the coupled physics.
