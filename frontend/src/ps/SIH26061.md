# SIH26061 — AI-Driven Smart Energy Management System for Polar Research Stations

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Clean & Green Technology
- **Difficulty Score:** 6.5/10

## What the PS is about
Polar research stations (Maitri/Bharati context) run under extreme conditions where diesel dependency is costly and renewables behave unpredictably. Energy use needs intelligent optimization.

## What to build
An intelligent energy-management system using AI for:
- Load forecasting under extreme polar conditions
- Renewable energy integration (solar/wind availability is highly seasonal)
- Fuel optimization and generator/renewable dispatch planning

## Key challenges
Modeling extreme-condition energy behavior with limited real station data; simulation-based approach is realistic.

## Why this difficulty
Short PS with clear objective — an ML forecasting/optimization problem with simulated data; moderate scope.
