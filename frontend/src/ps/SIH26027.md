# SIH26027 — AI-Powered Automatic Block Planning to Maximize Asset Availability (Indian Railways)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Railways
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Transportation & Logistics
- **Difficulty Score:** 6.5/10

## What the PS is about
Engineering, Traction Distribution, and S&T departments plan maintenance blocks independently and manually via BDMS, causing poor coordination, wasted block windows and reduced asset availability for train operations. Maintenance data lives separately in TMS, SMMS, TDMS; corridor availability in COA.

## What to build
An automatic block-planning AI system that:
- Integrates defects/overdue maintenance from TMS, SMMS, TDMS with corridor block availability from COA and train timetable/goods forecast
- Uses AI/ML to prioritize maintenance by criticality, urgency and impact on availability
- Optimizes block schedules (multi-department coordination) to minimize downtime
- Produces weekly and monthly block plans over multiple horizons

## Key challenges
Scheduling optimization (constraint-heavy combinatorial problem), integrating simulated/multi-source railway data.

## Why this difficulty
An optimization + integration problem with clear formulation; manageable for strong teams but requires genuine OR/ML modeling, not just CRUD.
