# SIH26090 — AI-Driven Market Linkage & Smart Cataloging App for Marginalized Artisans (MoSJE)

- **Organization:** Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (MoSJE)
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Heritage & Culture
- **Difficulty Score:** 5.5/10

## What the PS is about
Micro-entrepreneurs/artisans/weavers get concessional credit and physical exhibitions (Shilp Samagam, Surajkund, Dilli Haat) but no year-round digital market access — blocked by low digital literacy, language barriers and poor product presentation skills.

## What to build
An AI "virtual business manager" mobile app:
1. **AI Image Enhancer/Studio:** auto background removal, lighting correction, e-commerce-standard product photos
2. **Multilingual Auto-Cataloger:** voice-note product descriptions in regional languages → SEO-friendly English/Hindi listings via NLP
3. **Dynamic Pricing Assistant:** ML suggesting competitive prices from image + description + market trends and raw-material costs
- Cross-platform app + scalable backend, minimalist low-literacy-friendly UX, connection to B2B buyers/government e-marketplaces

## Key challenges
Solid CV (background removal/enhancement), vernacular voice NLP, pricing-signal data.

## Why this difficulty
Composeable from known AI services; the craft is in UX for low-literacy users and tying the three AI pieces together.
