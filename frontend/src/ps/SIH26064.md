# SIH26064 — Low-Cost Deployable Seafloor Metal Detection Sensor for Ocean Resource Exploration

- **Organization:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)
- **Category:** Hardware | **Theme:** Robotics and Drones
- **Difficulty Score:** 8.5/10

## What the PS is about
Deep-ocean mineral exploration (polymetallic nodules, hydrothermal sulphides, cobalt crusts, REE sediments) needs expensive survey tools. A cheap, vessel-deployable seafloor sensor would democratize exploration.

## What to build
A low-cost, deployable ocean-bottom sensor that:
- Is released from a research vessel during surveys
- Detects and maps metal-rich seabed deposits
- Provides rapid, cost-effective prospecting data for deep-ocean minerals

## Key challenges
Deep-sea pressure tolerance, underwater sensing physics (magnetometry/gradiometry or geochemical sensing), power and data recovery from the seafloor.

## Why this difficulty
Marine instrumentation for real deployment — pressure housings, underwater sensing and recovery logistics are genuinely hard engineering.
