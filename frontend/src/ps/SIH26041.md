# SIH26041 — AR-Based Vocational Training Simulator for Industrial Safety (Jharkhand Mining & Manufacturing)

- **Organization:** Government of Jharkhand
- **Category:** Software | **Theme:** Smart Education
- **Difficulty Score:** 6/10

## What the PS is about
Classroom safety training in Jharkhand's mines/steel/mica sectors has <20% retention; live drills are disruptive; VR headsets are unaffordable. Many accident victims had <30 days of orientation, and no standardized multilingual digital training exists.

## What to build
A mobile AR training & certification platform on mid-range Android (no headset):
- AR modules overlaid on real surroundings for fire/explosion response, gas leak & confined-space protocol, machinery safety (5 domains total)
- Assessment engine + QR-based certificate generation and verification
- Hindi and Santali localization; offline functionality
- Web admin compliance dashboard
- Deliverable: working APK with ≥2 complete modules, demo video, GitHub repo

## Key challenges
AR content authoring on mid-range phones, offline AR, Santali localization.

## Why this difficulty
AR on Android (ARCore) is well-documented and the scope is explicitly defined — the effort is content creation and polish, not research.
